No daily boosted values

Players can select 10 battles each day to receive boosted values. Each battle can be boosted to ×3 points. Before each battle, player can view all the waves and toggle whether the boost should apply.

A formation with the challenge wave can be worth about 30,000 points. If players do 10 challenge battles each day with boosted values, they can earn about 1.2M points just from 40 battles.

Formation Rework

Every wave is drawn, not scripted. A formation used to be one of ten fixed battles. Now, it is built wave by wave, so wave 2 of one battle can follow wave 1 of another.

Wave 1-4Each wave is drawn from the matching wave of all 10 battles, independently and uniformly.
Wave 5Challenge wave · 10% chanceChance of a fifth, challenge wave appeared after wave 4. Only 1 challenge wave in Battle 10 to sample from.

The pool

Click any wave to pick it for that slot. Dimmed waves were not drawn.
